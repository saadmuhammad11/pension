The circular already requires what this module does. Para 2: “All relevant observations must be pointed out in a single instance, in the claim rejection form signed by the Accounts Officer.” Para 3: a second return requires review and signature by the DAG (Funds) / DAO I.

What the eight cases have in common

All eight cases were read in full, 50 pages. Each carries at least one hard stop, and one defect recurs almost everywhere.

7 / 8
cases where the old GP Fund account number is missing, struck through, replaced by a receipt token, or answered with the personnel number. Rejection reason B2-vii.
6 / 8
cases where the amount claimed cannot be reconciled to anything on the file. No ledger, no balance statement, no Re-Calculation Sheet. Rejection reason B2-vi.
0 / 8
cases that would clear pre-audit as submitted. Every one is returnable today. The question is whether it is returned once, with everything on it, or four times.
